# Dimensional Upgrade

An Uplink Mast upgrade: every Cargo Link on that wall works anywhere, in any dimension (it includes Infinite Range). Transfers cost four times the energy. The wall must be loaded, so pair it with a Network Anchor. Right-click the mast with it, or open the mast and put it in the slot.
