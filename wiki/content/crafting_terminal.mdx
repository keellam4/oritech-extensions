# Crafting Terminal

A Cargo Controller terminal with a 3 × 3 crafting grid. Place it against any vault of a Cargo Wall.

## Using it

- After each craft the grid refills itself from the wall; shift-click the result to craft as many as the wall has ingredients for.
- With JEI installed, click "+" on a crafting recipe to fill the grid from the wall and your inventory. Ingredients you have none of turn red.
- It needs power: connect a cable. Each craft uses 100 RF (config: `terminalEnergyPerCraft`, 0 turns it off). The bar beside the result shows its energy. The Cargo Controller needs no power.
- The orange X puts the grid back into the wall; so does closing the screen.
