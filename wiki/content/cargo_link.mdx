# Cargo Link

A handheld terminal for your Cargo Wall. It opens the wall's crafting terminal from wherever you are, as long as you're in range.

## Using it

- Sneak + right-click a Cargo Controller or Crafting Terminal to link it. The tooltip shows where it's linked.
- Right-click anywhere to open the terminal, with the crafting grid. The action bar shows the signal, distance and battery.
- Range: 32 blocks from the controller with no mast, 64 with an Uplink Mast on the wall, plus 16 for each Antenna Segment on the mast (up to 8, 192 blocks).
- Each transfer uses a little of its 200,000 RF battery (5 RF per item, a bucket counts as 64). Charge it with an Induction Pylon or any charger.
- The terminal closes if you walk out of range or put the Link away.
