# Transparent Fluid Pipe

A see-through fluid pipe with a line through its centre, so it's easy to tell from the transparent item pipe. It connects to Oritech fluid pipes and machines like any fluid pipe, and paints with them.

You can see the fluid inside: while a pipe end moves fluid, every connected Transparent Fluid Pipe shows it, and it drains a couple of seconds after the flow stops.
