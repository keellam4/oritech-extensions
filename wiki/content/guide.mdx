# Oritech Extensions Guide

## Using it

- With [Oracle Index](https://modrinth.com/mod/oracle-index) installed, the guide opens straight to this wiki. The second time you open it in a session, it goes back to the page you were reading.
- Without Oracle Index, it puts a link to the online version of this wiki in chat.

You can also press **H** (Oracle Index's wiki key) at any time, or hold **Alt** over an item's tooltip to jump to its page.
