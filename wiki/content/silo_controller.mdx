# Silo Controller

The heart of a Fluid Silo: a solid 3 × 3 column of Silo Casing and Silo Glass, 3 to 9 blocks tall, with this controller in the bottom layer. It holds 1,000 buckets per block of the silo.

## Using it

- Right-click it for its screen: the fluids, their limits, reserves, locks and void overflow, and the partition upgrade slot (4, 9 or any number of fluids).
- Buckets and fluid pipes work on any block of the silo; touching a Cargo Wall, it joins the network.
- A mined controller keeps its fluids, settings and upgrade.
