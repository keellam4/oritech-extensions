# Hangar Door Extension

Oritech's Industrial Hangar Door is 3 blocks long. Each **Hangar Door Extension** adds 1 block. Stack them on the tip of the door (or on the previous extension).

## Limits

- Default maximum length: **16** blocks (Oritech's 3 + 13 extensions).
- Configurable: `maxHangarDoorLength` in `config/oritech_extensions-common.toml` (range 4–32).

## Behaviour

- Copies the door's direction, rotation, open/closed state, and paint colour.
- Opens and closes with the door, including linked doors.
- Breaking the door or any extension drops every extension past that point.

## Recipe

Shapeless: 1 Industrial Hangar Door → 3 Hangar Door Extensions.
