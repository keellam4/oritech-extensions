# Induction Pylon

A two-block pylon that wirelessly charges the energy items players carry within 8 blocks: armour first, then Curios slots, held items and the inventory. Feed energy into the bottom block.

## Using it

- Charging is fully efficient at the pylon and half as efficient at the edge of its range.
- A teal arc shows who it's charging. It glows and pulses when powered, and races with light while charging.
- Right-click to see its energy; sneak + right-click (owner) to choose who it charges: owner, team or everyone.
- Three styles: Tesla Spire, Coil Obelisk and Ring Pylon. Put one alone in a crafting grid to switch.
