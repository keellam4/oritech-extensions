# Rainbow Color Cartridge

Paints like a Color Cartridge, but the colour keeps cycling through the rainbow.

## Using it

- Right-click anything this mod paints: pipes, Industrial Glass, reactors, particle accelerators, vaults, Fluid Silos, Induction Pylons and the Uplink Mast. Load it into a Detailing Tool to paint with it there.
- Rainbow counts as its own colour, so rainbow pipes only connect to other rainbow pipes (and unpainted ones).
- It doesn't work on Oritech's own machines or on Oritech Things' addons yet.
