# Cargo Vault

Holds a lot of one item type (64 stacks by default). The item floats in the glass front with its count, and the neon strip above it fills as the vault does.

## Using it

- Right-click with items to put them in; right-click twice quickly, or with an empty hand, to put in every matching item you carry.
- Left-click the front to take a stack; sneak + left-click for one.
- Sneak + right-click with an empty hand (or right-click a side) for the upgrade screen and the lock.
- Touching vaults form a Cargo Wall: one Cargo Controller links them, and one cartridge paints them all.
- Breaking it keeps its items, upgrades and paint.
