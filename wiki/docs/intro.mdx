# Oritech Extensions

Small add-ons for [Oritech](https://modrinth.com/mod/oritech) on NeoForge 1.21.1.

## Features

- **[Hangar Door Extensions](@oritech_extensions:hangar_door_extension)**: make Industrial Hangar Doors longer than Oritech's default 3 blocks.
- **[Paintable machine addons](features/paintable_addons)**: use Color Cartridges on Oritech (and Oritech Things) addon blocks.
- **[Paintable standard pipes](features/paintable_pipes)**: paint whole connected item or fluid pipe networks.
- **[Paintable glass, reactors and accelerators](features/paintable_structures)**: one click paints a whole reactor, accelerator or wall of Industrial Glass.
