# Oritech Extensions

Small add-ons for [Oritech](https://modrinth.com/mod/oritech) on Minecraft 1.21.1, for NeoForge and Fabric.

## Features

- **[Hangar Door Extensions](@oritech_extensions:hangar_door_extension)**: make Industrial Hangar Doors longer than Oritech's default 3 blocks.
- **[Paintable machine addons](features/paintable_addons)**: use Color Cartridges on Oritech (and Oritech Things) addon blocks.
- **[Paintable pipes](features/paintable_pipes)**: paint whole item, fluid, energy and superconductor networks, framed and duct versions included.
- **[Paintable glass, reactors, accelerators and supports](features/paintable_structures)**: one click paints a whole reactor, accelerator, wall of Industrial Glass or beam-and-girder structure.
