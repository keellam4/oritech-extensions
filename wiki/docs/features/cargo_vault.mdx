# Cargo Vault

A Cargo Vault holds a lot of one item type. The item floats in its glass front with the count underneath, and the neon strip above the item lights up from left to right as the vault fills, so you can read a whole wall of vaults at a glance.

## Crafting

Shaped: Steel Ingot, Industrial Glass, Steel Ingot / Nickel Ingot, Barrel, Nickel Ingot / Steel Ingot, Adamant Ingot, Steel Ingot.

## Using it

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right-click with items | Put the held stack in |
| Right-click twice quickly, or with an empty hand | Put in every matching item from your inventory |
| Left-click the front | Take a stack |
| Sneak + left-click the front | Take one item |
| Sneak + right-click with an empty hand (or right-click a side) | Open the upgrade screen, with the lock |
| Right-click with an upgrade | Put it straight into its slot |

A **locked** vault (the padlock in the upgrade screen) keeps its item types when it's empty, so it only ever takes that item. The count turns orange while it's locked. Break a vault from any side but the front (or when it's empty).

## Size

64 stacks of its item by default: 4,096 of an item that stacks to 64, 1,024 of one that stacks to 16. Server owners can change it with `cargoVaultStacks` in the config.

## Dual, Quad and Grid Vaults

The **Dual Vault** has 2 windows, the **Quad Vault** 4 and the **Grid Vault** 16, each holding its own item type. They share the vault's space: with the default config, a Dual window holds 32 stacks, a Quad window 16 and a Grid window 4. Click the window you want: right-click puts items into it, left-click takes from it. An item type only ever lives in one window, so a second stack of iron joins the iron window instead of starting a new one.

Crafting: two Cargo Vaults stacked make a Dual Vault, four in a square make a Quad Vault. Four Quad Vaults in the corners, Steel Ingots on the sides and a Processing Unit in the middle make a Grid Vault.

## Compacting Vault

The **Compacting Vault** stores one item in up to three forms, worked out from crafting recipes the first time something goes in: iron nuggets, ingots and blocks; clay balls and clay; and so on. The wide top window shows the biggest form, the two below the smaller ones, all counting the same stock. Put in any form and take out any form: take a block and the ingot and nugget counts drop to match. It holds as much as a Cargo Vault holds of the middle form (4,096 ingots by default). An item with no such recipes is simply kept in the top window.

The **Dual Compacting Vault** works the same with two forms (ingots and blocks, say), for items that only compact once.

Each step can be a 3 × 3 or a 2 × 2 recipe, but it must also craft back: vanilla Quartz Blocks can't be turned back into quartz, so quartz stays a single form.

Crafting: Steel Ingot, Piston, Steel Ingot / Motor, Cargo Vault, Motor / Steel Ingot, Piston, Steel Ingot. The Dual Compacting Vault has Steel Ingots instead of the bottom Piston.

## Fluid Vault

The **Fluid Vault** holds one fluid: 256 buckets by default (`fluidVaultBuckets`), more with capacity upgrades. The fluid fills its glass from the bottom and the neon strip shows how full it is. Right-click it with a bucket or any fluid tank item to fill or empty it; fluid pipes connect like to any tank. It takes the same upgrades: Pull and Push move up to a bucket a second, Void deletes overflow of its fluid, and the lock keeps its fluid type when empty. Breaking it keeps the fluid.

Crafting: Steel Ingot, Industrial Glass, Steel Ingot / Fluid Pipe, Portable Tank, Fluid Pipe / Steel Ingot, Industrial Glass, Steel Ingot.

## Cargo Controller

A **Cargo Controller** links every item vault touching it, and every vault touching those, into one inventory (a Cargo Wall, up to 1,024 vaults). Fluid Vaults join too: items go to item vaults, fluids to Fluid Vaults. A [Fluid Silo](fluid_silo) whose controller touches the wall joins as one big tank.

- **Pipes:** connect a pipe to the controller instead of to each vault. Items go to the vault that already holds them first, then to a vault with room for a new item type, so one full window never jams the pipe. Pulling from the controller takes from any vault.
- **Terminal:** right-click the controller to see everything in the wall with totals.
  - Left-click an item to take a stack, right-click to take one, shift-click to send a stack to your inventory.
  - With items on your cursor, left-click the grid to put them all in, right-click to put one in. Shift-click items in your inventory to put them in.
  - Fluids show with their amount in buckets: click one with an empty bucket to fill it, or with a full bucket of that fluid to empty it in. Fluid pipes on the controller reach every Fluid Vault.
  - Type in the search box to filter by name, or `@mod` to filter by mod.
  - The buttons on the right sort the list (by amount, name or mod), switch between ascending and descending, and show items and fluids, only items or only fluids. Right-click a button to step back.
- The controller paints with its wall.

Crafting: Steel Ingot, Processing Unit, Steel Ingot / Magnetic Coil, Cargo Vault, Magnetic Coil / Steel Ingot, Item Pipe, Steel Ingot.

## Crafting Terminal

The **Crafting Terminal** reaches the same wall as a Cargo Controller: place it against any vault. Right-click it for the terminal with a 3 × 3 crafting grid underneath.

- Fill the grid from the list or your inventory, then take the result. After each craft, the grid refills itself from the wall.
- Shift-click the result to keep crafting until the wall runs out of ingredients.
- The orange X button puts the grid back into the wall. Closing the screen does the same.
- It paints with its wall.

Crafting: Steel Ingot, Processing Unit, Steel Ingot / Crafting Table, Cargo Controller, Crafting Table / Steel Ingot, Magnetic Coil, Steel Ingot.

## Upgrades

Sneak + right-click a vault with an empty hand (or right-click its side or top) to open the upgrade screen. It has one slot per kind of upgrade, so a vault never takes two Voids. You can also right-click the vault holding an upgrade to put it straight in. Upgrades stay in the vault when you break it.

| Upgrade | Slot takes | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Capacity (Steel ×2, Electrum ×4, Energite ×8, Duratium ×16, Prometheum ×32) | 5, of one tier | More room in every window; they add up, so five Electrum make ×20. They can't come out while the vault holds more than it could without them. |
| Void | 1 | Items that don't fit are deleted instead of backing up, but only items the vault already holds. |
| Pull | 1 | Once a second, pulls the vault's items from the inventories on the sides you pick. |
| Push | 1 | Once a second, pushes the vault's items into the inventories on the sides you pick. |
| Redstone | 1 | The vault gives off a redstone signal as strong as it is full. A redstone signal into the vault pauses Pull and Push. |

Pull and Push each have a small unfolded cube of the vault's sides (Front, Back, Left, Right, Up, Down) in the screen: click a side to turn it on or off. Several sides can be on at once. Both start on the back.

Crafting: each Capacity upgrade is four of its ingot around the tier below (Steel around a Chest). Void: Obsidian around Enderic Compound. Pull: Hopper over Steel, Motor, Steel. Push: Dropper over Steel, Motor, Steel. Redstone: Comparator over Steel, Block of Redstone, Steel.

## Readout

Aim at a window and a small panel under the crosshair shows its item and how many it holds out of how many fit, plus "Locked" when the vault is locked.

## Pipes, hoppers and comparators

Pipes, hoppers and other mods see a vault as one slot holding its item. Items only go into a vault that holds the same item (or an empty, unlocked one). A comparator reads how full it is, from 0 (empty) to 15 (full).

## Breaking it

A broken vault keeps everything: its items, its lock and its paint are stored on the item, and come back when you place it again.

## Paint

The orange frame takes paint, including Rainbow. Touching vaults form a **Cargo Wall** and are painted together with one cartridge or one click of the [Detailing Tool](detailing_tool). A new vault placed against a painted wall takes its colour, unless the vault's item is already painted.
