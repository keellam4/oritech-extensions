# Cargo Vault

A Cargo Vault holds a lot of one item type. The item floats in its glass front with the count underneath, and the neon strip above the item lights up from left to right as the vault fills, so you can read a whole wall of vaults at a glance.

## Crafting

Shaped: Steel Ingot, Industrial Glass, Steel Ingot / Nickel Ingot, Barrel, Nickel Ingot / Steel Ingot, Adamant Ingot, Steel Ingot.

## Using it

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| Right-click with items | Put the held stack in |
| Right-click twice quickly, or with an empty hand | Put in every matching item from your inventory |
| Left-click the front | Take a stack |
| Sneak + left-click the front | Take one item |
| Sneak + right-click with an empty hand | Lock or unlock the item type |

A **locked** vault keeps its item type when it's empty, so it only ever takes that item. The count turns orange while it's locked. Break a vault from any side but the front (or when it's empty).

## Size

64 stacks of its item by default: 4,096 of an item that stacks to 64, 1,024 of one that stacks to 16. Server owners can change it with `cargoVaultStacks` in the config.

## Dual, Quad and Grid Vaults

The **Dual Vault** has 2 windows, the **Quad Vault** 4 and the **Grid Vault** 16, each holding its own item type. They share the vault's space: with the default config, a Dual window holds 32 stacks, a Quad window 16 and a Grid window 4. Click the window you want: right-click puts items into it, left-click takes from it. An item type only ever lives in one window, so a second stack of iron joins the iron window instead of starting a new one.

Crafting: two Cargo Vaults stacked make a Dual Vault, four in a square make a Quad Vault. Four Quad Vaults in the corners, Steel Ingots on the sides and a Processing Unit in the middle make a Grid Vault.

## Compacting Vault

The **Compacting Vault** stores one item in up to three forms, worked out from crafting recipes the first time something goes in: iron nuggets, ingots and blocks; clay balls and clay; and so on. The wide top window shows the biggest form, the two below the smaller ones, all counting the same stock. Put in any form and take out any form: take a block and the ingot and nugget counts drop to match. It holds as much as a Cargo Vault holds of the middle form (4,096 ingots by default). An item with no such recipes is simply kept in the top window.

The **Dual Compacting Vault** works the same with two forms (ingots and blocks, say), for items that only compact once.

Each step can be a 3 × 3 or a 2 × 2 recipe, but it must also craft back: vanilla Quartz Blocks can't be turned back into quartz, so quartz stays a single form.

Crafting: Steel Ingot, Piston, Steel Ingot / Motor, Cargo Vault, Motor / Steel Ingot, Piston, Steel Ingot. The Dual Compacting Vault has Steel Ingots instead of the bottom Piston.

## Readout

Aim at a window and a small panel under the crosshair shows its item and how many it holds out of how many fit, plus "Locked" when the vault is locked.

## Pipes, hoppers and comparators

Pipes, hoppers and other mods see a vault as one slot holding its item. Items only go into a vault that holds the same item (or an empty, unlocked one). A comparator reads how full it is, from 0 (empty) to 15 (full).

## Breaking it

A broken vault keeps everything: its items, its lock and its paint are stored on the item, and come back when you place it again.

## Paint

The orange frame takes paint, including Rainbow. Touching vaults form a **Cargo Wall** and are painted together with one cartridge or one click of the [Detailing Tool](detailing_tool). A new vault placed against a painted wall takes its colour, unless the vault's item is already painted.
