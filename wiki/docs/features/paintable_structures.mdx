# Paintable Glass, Reactors and Accelerators

Color Cartridges also paint **Industrial Glass**, the **nuclear reactor** and the **particle accelerator**. One click paints the whole build, so a reactor or accelerator matches the machines around it.

## What gets painted together

| Click on | Paints |
|---|---|
| Industrial Glass | All Industrial Glass touching it |
| Any reactor block | The whole reactor: walls, controller, rods, vents, heat pipes, condensers, reflectors and every port |
| Any accelerator block | The whole accelerator: controller, guide rings, motors, sensors and particle collectors. Parts up to 8 blocks apart in a straight line count as one accelerator, so gaps between rings are fine |

**Orange** clears the colour again.

## What changes colour

- Industrial Glass: the frame. The glass itself stays clear.
- Reactor walls: the coloured trim. Reactor parts: their coloured trim, and heat pipes all over.
- Accelerator parts: the red-pink casing and orange trim.
- Status lights don't change: redstone indicators, yellow markers and the cyan particle lights keep their colours, so a painted build reads the same.

## Rules

- Colour is **world-only**, like pipes. Broken blocks drop unpainted.
- A new block takes the colour of the painted blocks it joins, if they all have the same colour.
- One click paints at most 4096 blocks (`maxPaintGroupSize` in the config). Bigger builds take another click.

## Config

| Key | Default | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `pipePaintCostMode` | `NETWORK` | `NETWORK` = one cartridge per click, whatever the size; `PER_BLOCK` = one per block |
| `maxPaintGroupSize` | `4096` | Most glass, reactor or accelerator blocks painted by one click |
